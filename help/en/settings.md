# Goals, plan and meals

Use it to set your goals and weight-loss plan and the Meals formula. The same page of the app also holds the tabs of **Your account** that are described at the end of this document: profiles and PIN, personal data, backups and export to PDF.

## How to open it

- On the computer, in the sidebar: **Goals, plan and meals** and **Your account**, which brings together the account, profiles and data in tabs: **Account** (with the **Personal data** panel at the end), **Profiles** and **Data and backups**.
- On mobile, with the settings icon in the top bar. You can also reach both from the profile button.
- The **✕** button in the top bar closes the page and returns you to the section and the point of the page where you were.

## Tabs

- **Goals, plan and meals** has two tabs: **Goals and plan** and **Meals**.
- **Your account** has three tabs: **Account** (with the **Personal data** panel at the end), **Profiles** and **Data and backups**.

The last tab opened in each group is remembered.

## Goals and plan

### Daily targets
They set the goal lines on the bars and count the days and nights on which you meet them (Activity and Sleep).

- **Steps per day** (1,000 to 50,000; default 8,000).
- **Minutes of exercise per day** (5 to 300; default 30).
- **Active calories per day** (50 to 3,000 kcal; default 400).
- **Sleep per night** in hours (4 to 12; default 7).
- **Target schedule**: bedtime and wake-up time on weekdays and at weekends (default 00:00 and 07:00).
- **Save targets** saves the changes; **Default values** resets them.

### Weight-loss plan
- **Starting weight**, **Final target** (lower than the starting weight) and **Plan start**.
- **Three weekly paces** (kg per week, greater than zero): the plan drops that much each week while you are above 1.35 times the target (first pace), above 1.2 times (second) and above the target (final pace). Defaults: 1, 0.75 and 0.5 kg per week.
- With this, the plan line, the milestones and the comparison in Summary and Weight are drawn.
- **Save plan**. What you change here is kept even if other values arrive.

## Meals

- **Meals · your dishes**: all the dishes you have logged, from most to least used.
  - Rename a dish, delete it, merge several into one (select several and press **Merge**) or add new dishes so they appear in the suggestions. There is a search box.
- **Meal score and estimated loss**: the weights of the score for the day and of the estimated loss.
  - **Weights** for breakfast, lunch, dinner, water and sport.
  - **Penalty** for food and drink indulgences.
  - **Default score** for an unscored meal (0 to 5; default 3).
  - **Estimated loss factor** (greater than 0 and up to 5).
  - Checkboxes: a normal day keeps your weight (neutral model); count all days even if you do not weigh yourself (using your last weigh-in); and count days without meals logged with the default score.
  - Formula: estimated loss for the day = factor × (goal − weight) ÷ goal × score for the day.
  - A preview shows the breakdown in % of each item.
  - **Save formula**, **Default values** (neutral model) and **Classic model** (the reference values the formula was first tuned with).

## Your account tabs
The following three sections are tabs of **Your account**, not of Goals, plan and meals. The rest of Your account (name, email, language, theme, password…) is described in [Accounts and access](accounts.md).

### Profiles (Your account → Profiles)

- Profiles are ways of using your account with access to only some modules: for example, one with Weight and Meals to hand your phone to someone without them seeing the rest. **All profiles see the same data, your account's**, and each one can have its own PIN. You switch profile with the profile button (initials and name) next to the title in the top bar, which also offers **Goals, plan and meals**, **Your account** (where profiles are managed), **Administration** (if you are an administrator), **Lock profile** (if the profile has a PIN) and **Sign out**.
- Each profile row lets you set:
  - **Name** and **colour** (the colour and initials identify the profile).
  - **Set PIN** / **Change PIN** / **Remove PIN**: 4-digit PIN for the profile.
  - **Modules** (every profile, the main one included): tap each module to give the profile access to it or take it away: Summary, Weight, Body, Activity, Sleep, Meals, Intimate, Weight history, **Goals, plan and meals**, **Your account** (where profiles, data and backups are managed) and, in administrator accounts, **Administration**. A profile only sees its modules in the menu, and opening another one takes it to the first module it has; Help is always available. **At least one profile must keep Goals, plan and meals, Your account and (for administrators) Administration**, so the account can always be managed; the other profiles can have whichever modules you like.
  - **Delete** (asks for confirmation with "Sure?"): removes the profile; the data stays, since it is your account's. The main profile (the first one) cannot be deleted; move another one to the top first.
- **Add profile**: type a name and press **Add profile**. Switch to it with the profile button.
- **Order and main profile:** drag a profile by its **⋮⋮** handle to change the order. The first one in the list is the **main profile**, and the order decides which profile opens when you open the app (see Profile PIN).

### Profile PIN
- It is a 4-digit screen lock with a numeric keypad; it opens as soon as you type the fourth digit. It **does not encrypt the data.**
- **The PIN decides the profile:** on the lock screen, the PIN you type opens the profile that has it. So each person can enter with their own PIN and see only the modules of their profile.
- **Shared PIN:** if several profiles have the same PIN, entering with it opens them together and you see the modules of all of them. When you set a PIN that another profile already has, the panel tells you so.
- **Which profile opens:** the order of the list decides it. When you open the app, if the **first profile** (the main one) has no PIN, you go straight into it; if it has a PIN, the lock screen asks for it and the PIN typed decides which profile opens. After 5 failed attempts you have to wait 30 seconds.
- The lock screen also lets you **Sign in with another profile**.
- **Lock profile** (in the profile menu) asks for the PIN again. A profile with a PIN also **locks itself after some minutes without activity** (1 by default; it is changed in Your account → Account → Profile).
- **Each profile reopens where it was:** entering a profile shows the section it had open last time, and its tabs until the PIN lock shows (then every module starts again from its first tab).
- Cycle and intimacy no longer have a PIN of their own: who sees them depends on whether the profile has the **Intimate** module.

### Personal data (Your account → Account, at the end)
- **Height (cm)**: between 120 and 230. It is used for BMI, lean mass index, reference zones and the tape measurement formulas.
- **Sex for the formulas**: Not specified, Male or Female. It is used for fat zones, lean mass index, VO2 max and the tape measurement formulas.
- **Date of birth**: refines the fat and VO2 max ranges to your age band.
- Press **Save profile**.

### Data and backups (Your account → Data and backups)

#### Apple Health / Android
- The first card says that sleep, activity, workouts, resting heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen, breathing rate, VO2 max, ECG and watch alerts arrive on their own from the phone: from the iPhone (Apple Health, with the Health Auto Export app) or from Android (Health Connect, with the BodyLens app). Both use the same sending address.
- It has two links: **Data sources ›** (goes to Your account → Data sources) and **Step-by-step guide ›** (opens the [Sync](sync.md) help).
- ECG, heart alerts, wrist temperature, State of Mind, symptoms and medications are only sent by the iPhone (Apple Health); on Android, log them by hand in the app.

#### Source of the data
- Check figures: **days saved**, **last weigh-in** and when the data was last read.
- Data from the scale and the phone (Apple Health or Android) arrives on its own at the server every few minutes; the cloud button at the top asks for it at once. You can see the sources in Your account.
- Weigh-ins and composition come from the **Withings** scale; sleep, activity and workouts mainly from **Apple Health** or **Android**.

#### Backup
- **Download full copy**: saves a JSON file with everything (weigh-ins, composition, measurements, activity, sleep, workouts, meals, Health data and settings of the current profile). Its name includes the date.
- **Import a backup…**: choose a backup file. A summary is shown (date of the backup and number of days of each type) and two options:
  - **Merge with the current data**: adds what is missing and, if a day is in both places, the backup's version is kept.
  - **Replace all**: leaves your data exactly as in the backup. It asks for an extra confirmation ("Yes, replace all").
  - You can also **Cancel**. If the file is not a backup from this app, you are told.
- **History (CSV)** and **Measurements (CSV)**: export your weight history and your tape measurements as CSV for spreadsheets.

## Export to PDF

- It is opened with the **PDF** icon in the top bar (in Your account → Data and backups and in Goals, plan and meals it offers to export everything).
- Options:
  - **Period**: 1 wk, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year or All; it starts with the one of the section you are in.
  - **Orientation**: Portrait or Landscape.
  - **What to export**: **This tab**, **Choose** (a grid by modules and sections, with **Select all** and **Remove all**) or **All**.
- Press **See preview**: A4 pages are generated with the charts as you see them in the app, with the profile name and the period in the header. Tap a page to enlarge it.
- **Download PDF** saves the file; **Change options** goes back.
- The PDF is always made with the light theme.
- A profile only exports the modules it has.
