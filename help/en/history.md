# History

Use it to see your whole weight journey, year by year, and to compare your weight-loss attempts over time. Weigh-ins come from the **Withings** scale and from any manual or historical weigh-ins you have imported.

## Tabs

- **Years**, **All** and **Attempts**. The app remembers the last one you opened.
- On phones, the explanation of a chart card is behind the ⓘ next to its title: tap the title to show it.

## Period

- The floating selector in this section is its own: **6 months**, **1 year**, **3 years**, **5 years**, **10 years** and **All**. It is not shared with the other sections, which use shorter periods.

## Years

- **Year on year**:
  - **Chart**: one bar per year with the weight range (minimum to maximum) and a dot with the average. Hovering or swiping over a bar shows the average, the range and the number of days with a weigh-in. Only the years within the chosen period are shown.
  - **Table**: year and number of days, average and range, change between the first and last weigh-in of the year and, if there is scale data, average fat and muscle.

## All

- **Your whole history**:
  - **Summary**: number of days with a weigh-in, date of the first one, all-time maximum and minimum and, if you have imported older data, since when your Withings account has data.
  - **Chart**: daily weigh-in and trend (exponential smoothing), with green bands over the weight-loss attempts. Tapping a legend item hides or shows it. With **All** you see everything from the first weigh-in; with another period, only that stretch.

## Attempts

- **Your weight-loss attempts**:
  - **What counts as an attempt**: each drop of at least 4 kg sustained for 4 weeks or more, from the peak to the lowest point. They are detected on the trend line of your whole history.
  - **Comparison chart**: each line starts at 0 at the peak and goes down with the kilos lost, week by week. The dot marks the minimum and the line continues for 6 more months to show the rebound. Hovering or swiping shows, for each attempt, the weight and the change in that week. A label per attempt is drawn on the right.
  - **Table**: one row per attempt with its period, duration in weeks, pace in kg per week, start and end weight, change and **rebound at 6 months**. The labels **current**, **latest** and **best** mark the attempt in progress, the most recent finished one and the one that lost the most kilos. The **rebound** is the maximum rise of the trend in the 6 months after the lowest point ("n/a" appears if there is no data yet).
  - **Showing or hiding attempts**: tap a row in the table. By default the current, latest and best are shown (the best is always green). Newly detected attempts start hidden unless they are the current, latest or best.
  - The chosen period filters the attempts that fall (or end) within it. If there is only data for the current attempt, this is stated.
