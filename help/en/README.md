# BodyLens help

One file per module. The sections of the app are those in the navigation bar; the account ones are in "Your account".

## The app
| Module | What it is |
|---|---|
| [Summary](summary.md) | Overview: trend weight, "Today at a glance", body, health and analysis |
| [Weight](weight.md) | Weight progress, plan, weeks and calendar |
| [Body](body.md) | Scale (composition), tape measurements, heart and wellbeing |
| [Activity](activity.md) | Steps, calories, exercise, workouts and analysis |
| [Sleep](sleep.md) | Last night, sleep balance, progress, schedule, naps and the rules for nights |
| [Meals](meals.md) | Meal log and score, and their effect on your weight |
| [Intimate](intimate.md) | Cycle, sexuality and mood, for the profiles that have the Intimate module |
| [History](history.md) | Your whole weight history by year and weight-loss attempts |
| [Goals, plan and meals](settings.md) | Goals and weight-loss plan, Meals formula; also profiles, PIN, personal data, backups and export to PDF (in "Your account") |

On a phone, each tab starts with a chart or its figures; the explanation of a chart (and of the cards of Your account, Administration and Goals, plan and meals) waits behind the **ⓘ** next to its title (tap the title to show or hide it), and the cards to enter data by hand are at the end of the tab.
On touch screens, swipe sideways to go to the next or previous tab; past the last tab of a module you go on to the next module (and before the first, back to the previous one).

On a phone:

- The **bottom bar** shows 4 modules and **More**, a menu with every module: tap its name to open it (on the tab you last used), or its arrow › to unfold its tabs and go straight to one (tap the arrow again to fold them). Tap **More** again, ✕, outside the menu or swipe it down to close it. By default the bar shows the 4 modules you use most on that device (worked out when you open the app, so the bar does not move while you use it). **Edit the bar** (in More) turns **Most used** off and lets you choose them: remove one with − and add another with +; with the bar full, remove one first. With 5 modules or fewer, the bar shows them all.
- The **tabs of each module** are at the bottom, in a strip tinted with the module's colour, just above the bottom bar of modules, within reach of your thumb. **Your account** and **Administration** show theirs there too, with their icons, and a sideways swipe goes through them.
- When you scroll down, the title gives way to a **thin bar** at the top with the module's name; tap it to go back to the top. In Administration that bar also shows its tabs.
- The **period** (1 week, 1 month…) shows only the one in use; tap it to choose another.
- When you change tab, a few **dots** show where you are among the module's tabs.
