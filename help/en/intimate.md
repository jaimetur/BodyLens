# Cycle and intimacy

A private section for tracking the menstrual cycle, sexual activity and mood. It is protected by its own privacy PIN and can be hidden completely.

## Source of the data

- Cycle, sexual activity and mood records come from **Apple Health** (iPhone, through Health Auto Export) or **Android** (Health Connect, through the BodyLens app), or you log them here by hand. Android sends menstruation and sexual activity; **mood (State of Mind) is only sent by the iPhone, so on Android log it by hand in the app.** Imported records are labelled "Apple Health / Android".

## Who sees it

- Intimate is a module like the others: a profile sees it only if it has the **Intimate** module (Your account → Profiles). There is no separate private PIN any more; to keep it private, give the other profiles a PIN and leave Intimate out of their modules.

## Tabs

- **Cycle**, **Sexuality** and **Mood**. The app remembers the last one you opened.

## Cycle

- **Current status**: ring with the cycle day and phase (Period, Follicular phase, Estimated fertile window, Estimated ovulation day, Luteal phase or Late period), when the next period is expected and, if there is not enough data, a notice that a 28-day cycle is being used.
- **Current cycle line**: period, fertile window and ovulation, with a mark on today.
- **Figures**: next period, ovulation, fertile window, average cycle (with its range), average period (from the last 6 periods) and last period.
- **Monthly calendar**: navigate with ‹ and ›. Colours: period, predicted period, fertile window and ovulation. A dot marks the days with sexual activity logged.
  - **Tapping a day** marks or unmarks it as a period day.
  - **Pressing and holding** (or right-clicking with a mouse) opens its editing: No period, Light, Moderate or Heavy.
- **Log a period**: start date, duration (2 to 10 days) and intensity; or **My period started today**.
- **Cycle history**: the latest cycles with their length. Tapping one lets you change the start, the end (no more than 15 days of period) and the intensity, or delete it.

### Calculation rules
- The average cycle is obtained from cycles between 15 and 60 days long; without enough data, 28 is used.
- **Ovulation** is estimated by subtracting 14 days from the next period, and the **fertile window** runs from 5 days before ovulation to 1 day after.
- These are approximate estimates: they are not suitable as a contraceptive method or for planning pregnancy precisely.

## Sexuality

- **Sexual activity**: monthly calendar where a tap on a day logs or removes it; pressing and holding lets you adjust the time, whether there was protection (with, without or unspecified) and log another one on the same day. If there are several on one day, their number is shown.
- Figures: last 30 days, this year, per week (12 months) and percentage with protection; bars for the last 12 months.
- **Other records**: form to log sexual activity or intermenstrual bleeding with date and time; the latest records can be edited or deleted.
- If the profile has a linked partner, sexual activity is shared between both (the partner's activity before the relationship start date is not counted).

## Mood

- **Mood**: your latest record (face, description, emotions and what influences it) and the **How are you feeling now?** row, with seven faces from "Very unpleasant" to "Very pleasant" to log your mood with one tap.
- **Scale**: from −3 (very unpleasant) to +3 (very pleasant).
- If there are no records yet, it explains how to import them from Apple Health on the iPhone (Health app → Mindfulness → State of mind) with the Health Auto Export automation, or you can log them here; on Android, log them here by hand.
- **Period**: the floating selector (1 wk to All) appears only on this tab.
- **In numbers**: average mood, pleasant and unpleasant days and comparison with the previous period.
- **Progress**: mood for each day and its 7-day average.
- **Calendar**: the mood of each day by colour.
- **What you feel and what influences it**: most frequent emotions, average mood by what influences it (work, family, partner…) and the relationship between hours slept and the next day's mood (and the reverse).
- **What relates to your mood** (whole history): for each metric, it compares your mood on days above and below normal. It needs at least 6 days with mood and with each metric. These are relationships, not causes.
- **What your days are like depending on mood**: average of each metric on pleasant, neutral and unpleasant days.
- **Your difficult days**: unpleasant days or worse, with what happened that day and the night before, and your trend weight over the following 7 days.
- **Latest records**.
