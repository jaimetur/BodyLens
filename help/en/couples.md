# Partner

Two accounts can be linked as a couple to **share Cycle and intimacy records**. All other data (weight, sleep, activity, meals…) stays individual.

## Linking the accounts
- In "Your account" → **Partner**, enter your partner's **username** and, optionally, the **Together since** date. Press **Request to link the accounts**.
- Your partner sees the request in their "Your account" and **accepts** or **rejects** it. If both of you request it at the same time, the accounts are linked straight away.
- Each account can be linked to only one other account.

## What is shared
- **Cycle**: period, intermenstrual bleeding, ovulation, basal temperature, cervical mucus, contraceptives and pregnancy.
- **Intimacy**: sexual activity records.
- Both of you see and **edit** those records; whatever one adds or deletes, the other sees in less than a minute.
- When you link, the records of those types that each of you already had are merged.
- Mood, symptoms and the rest of the Health records are **not** shared.

## Unlinking the accounts
- "Your account" → Partner → **Unlink the accounts**. Each of you keeps **a copy** of what you had shared; from then on nothing is shared.
