# Delete your BodyLens account

You can delete your account and all your data at any time, yourself, from the website or from the BodyLens app for Android.

## From the website or the app

1. Sign in to BodyLens.
2. Open **Your account** (from the avatar menu or the sidebar).
3. At the end of the page, in **Delete my account**, type your password and tap **Delete my account**.
4. Confirm. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out.

Before deleting it, if you want to keep your data, download a copy in **Your account → Data and backups**.

## What is deleted

- Your account (username, name, email and password) and all its data: weigh-ins, body composition, measurements, activity, workouts, sleep, heart, meals, cycle, intimacy, mood, wellbeing, notes, profiles and settings.
- The sending addresses of your phone and the connection with Withings stop working.
- If you have a subscription (website or Google Play), it is cancelled, so nothing more is charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; deleting the account does.
- If you had a linked partner, the link is removed and your partner keeps their own copy of the records you shared (cycle and intimacy).
- The data disappears from the database at once and from the server's backup copies as they expire, within 30 days.

## If you cannot sign in

Write to the contact address shown at the top of this page from the email address of your account (or explain how we can confirm the account is yours), asking for your account to be deleted. The request is handled within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

## Data on your phone

- Uninstall the BodyLens app for Android to remove what it keeps on the phone (the sending address and the date of the last sending). You can also revoke its permissions in Health Connect.
- Data in Health Connect, Apple Health or Withings belongs to those services: BodyLens does not delete it.
