# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

BodyLens is a personal health tracker that runs on a private server. This policy explains what data BodyLens keeps, where it comes from, what it is used for, who can see it and what your rights are. It applies to the website and to the BodyLens app for Android. The person or organisation responsible for your data (the controller) and how to contact them are shown at the top of this page.

## What BodyLens is

- Each BodyLens server is run by its administrator for a small group of people. Accounts are created only by invitation or by a request that the administrator approves.
- BodyLens has no advertising, does not sell or rent data, does not use your data to profile you for third parties and does not use analytics or tracking services.

## The data BodyLens keeps

- **Your account**: username, full name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash, never in plain text), language, text size, theme and your preferences in the app.
- **Personal data you enter**: height, sex for the formulas and date of birth, used for the body mass index, the lean mass index, reference ranges and tape-measure formulas.
- **Health and wellbeing data**: weight and body composition, tape measurements, activity (steps, distance, floors, calories, exercise minutes), workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, VO2 max, wrist temperature, ECG recordings and the watch's heart alerts, sleep (schedule, stages, naps), menstrual cycle and related records, sexual activity, mood and wellbeing records, meals and your notes.
- **Connections**: the sending address of your phone (it contains a secret key) and, if you connect Withings, the authorisation Withings gives BodyLens. Secret keys are stored encrypted on the server.
- **Subscription**: if you subscribed, the plan, its state and the end of the current period, and the reference that Stripe (website) or Google Play (app) gives to your subscription, to know whether it is active. Card and payment details are handled only by Stripe or Google; BodyLens never receives them.
- **Technical data**: your session (to keep you signed in), the date of your last data received and synchronisation results. The server keeps a basic access log for security; it does not record your health data, passwords or secret addresses.

## Where the data comes from

- **What you enter by hand** in BodyLens.
- **Withings**, only if you connect your Withings account: weigh-ins, body composition, activity, sleep, heart rate, SpO2, VO2 max and ECG.
- **Apple Health** (iPhone), through the Health Auto Export app you configure, which sends your data to your BodyLens sending address.
- **Health Connect** (Android), through the BodyLens app, as described below.

## Health Connect (Android)

- The BodyLens app for Android **only reads** from Health Connect; it never writes or deletes data there.
- It asks for permission to read: steps, distance, floors climbed, active and total calories, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate, VO2 max, sleep, exercise sessions, menstruation and sexual activity. You choose which ones to allow, and you can withdraw any permission at any time in Health Connect.
- If you also allow **reading in the background**, the app sends new data every hour with the app closed.
- The data read from Health Connect is sent **only to your own account on your BodyLens server**, over an encrypted connection, to show it to you in BodyLens. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not shared with third parties and is not used to decide on credit, insurance or employment.
- The use of information received from Health Connect complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## What the data is used for

- To show you your data, your progress, your goals and the analyses of BodyLens (for example, the sleep balance or the relation between your sleep and your weight).
- To send you the emails you need: invitations, access requests, couple requests and password resets.
- To keep the service working and secure (sessions, backups, protection against repeated failed sign-ins).

## Legal basis

- **Your explicit consent** for your health data: you give it when you create your account, connect a source or enter data. You can withdraw it at any time by disconnecting the source or deleting your account.
- **Providing the service you asked for** for your account data.
- **Legitimate interest** in keeping the service secure (access log, protection against attacks).

## Who can see your data

- **Only you.** Each account sees only its own data.
- **Your partner**, only if you both decide to link your accounts: you then share the cycle and intimacy records, and nothing else. You can unlink at any time.
- **Profiles** of your own account (for example, one with only some modules) show your own data to whoever you give that profile's PIN.
- **The administrator of the server** has technical access to the server where the data is stored, as anyone running a server does. The administrator does not look at your data except when you ask for help or when it is needed to keep the service working.
- **Withings** receives only what is needed to give BodyLens access to your Withings data when you connect it.
- **The email service** of the server sends the emails described above.
- **Stripe** (website) and **Google Play** (app) handle subscription payments; the server receives from them only the state of your subscription. Stripe receives your name and email to make the payment.
- Help texts may be translated by a translation service; only the help texts are sent, never your data.

## Where the data is stored and for how long

- In a database on the BodyLens server shown at the top of this page, not in third-party clouds. Connections to the server are encrypted (HTTPS).
- The server makes a daily backup copy and keeps the last 30.
- Your data is kept while your account exists. If a paid subscription or a free access ends and is not renewed, the account stays read-only for 30 days, you are warned three times by email (when it ends, a week before the deletion and the day before), and then the account is deleted.
- When an account is deleted, its data is deleted from the database at once and disappears from the backups as they expire (within 30 days).

## Cookies and storage on your device

- BodyLens uses only two cookies, both necessary: the **session** cookie, which keeps you signed in, and the **language** cookie. There are no advertising or analytics cookies.
- The app keeps a copy of your data and your preferences in your browser's storage so it opens quickly; signing out or deleting your account removes access to it.

## Your rights

- **Access and portability**: Your account → Data and backups lets you download a full copy of your data at any time.
- **Rectification**: you can change your data in the app.
- **Erasure**: Your account → Delete my account deletes your account and all its data. See also [how to delete an account](/delete-account).
- **Withdrawal of consent and objection**: disconnect a source, revoke the Health Connect or Withings permissions, or delete your account.
- To exercise any right, or for any question about privacy, write to the contact address shown at the top of this page. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Minors

BodyLens is not intended for people under 16.

## Security

Passwords are stored with a secure hash (argon2), secret keys are stored encrypted, every connection uses HTTPS and repeated failed sign-ins are blocked. Sending addresses contain a secret key: do not share them.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date. Important changes are announced in the app.
