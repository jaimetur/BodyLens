# Summary

This is the home screen: it brings together, at a glance, your trend weight, body composition, day-to-day data, health and the most relevant analysis from all the other sections.

## Sections

At the top there are five tabs that change what is shown:

- **General**: main card, "Today at a glance" and "The most important".
- **Body**: weight and composition cards, and "Your body today".
- **Day by day**: activity and sleep cards.
- **Health**: recovery and health cards (HRV, SpO2, breathing, VO2 max, cycle, alerts and ECG, wellbeing).
- **Analysis**: "What your data says", grouped by topic.

## Period

- A **floating selector** (1 wk, 1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, All) changes the trend line, the changes on each card and their mini charts.
- The chosen period is shared with the other sections that have a selector.

## Main card (General)

- **Trend weight**: this is not your last weigh-in, but a smoothed value of your weigh-ins (exponential moving average) to remove daily noise. Below it you see the last weigh-in and its difference from the previous one.
- **Chips**: change of the trend over 7 days and over the chosen period; if there is a plan, also the change since it started and the distance from the weight expected by the plan.
- **Ring**: percentage of the way covered between the starting weight and the goal, with marks at the plan's milestones. Below, kilos lost and kilos to go.
- **Trend line**: dots for each weigh-in, the trend line and, dashed, the plan line.

## Today at a glance (General)

Rows that appear only if there is data and that take you to their section when tapped:

- **Preparation** (0 to 100): a mix of last night's score (45 %), the 7-night sleep balance (30 %) and your sleeping heart rate compared with your recent nights (25 %). Labels: Low, Fair, Good, Excellent.
- **Last night's sleep**, **Sleep balance**, **HRV**, **Steps**, **Workouts** (last 7 days), **Meal** (score for the day), **Intimacy** and **Cycle** (these two only if you have unlocked Intimate).

## The most important

Up to four highlighted sentences: weight pace, recovery and anything in red or orange. The "See all analyses" link opens the Analysis tab.

## Body cards

- Weight, BMI (if you have entered your height), body fat %, fat mass, muscle, lean mass, water % and basal metabolism (if there is no height, bone is shown instead of BMI).
- Each card shows the current value, its change over the period and a mini chart. If the scale has not sent that value for a while, the card appears dimmed with the date of the last data.
- Tapping a card opens its enlarged detail.
- Weight and composition data come from the **Withings** scale.

## Your body today

- Breakdown of your weight into fat, muscle and bone (median of the last two weeks) and, if there is a plan, the same breakdown when you started it.
- **Quality of the loss**: what part of what you have lost since the start of the plan is fat and what part is lean mass. Ideally it is mostly fat.

## Day by day and health cards

- Steps, exercise minutes, sleep and similar (Day by day) and HRV, overnight SpO2, breathing, VO2 max, cycle, alerts and ECG, and wellbeing (Health).
- This data comes from **Apple Health** (through Health Auto Export); sleep and activity data can also come from Withings when Health does not have it.
- They show the average for the period and, if you have chosen a period, the difference from the previous period.

## What your data says

Sentences generated from your data, grouped into Weight, Meals, Activity, Sleep and recovery, Health and Intimacy. Each one has a label (**Good**, **To improve**, **Data**) according to the colour of the indicator. Among other things they compare:

- the pace of the last 4 weeks against the pace needed to reach the goal by the plan's date;
- your weight against today's plan;
- your current weight-loss attempt against the best previous one and the average rebound of the previous ones;
- the day of the week when you usually weigh the most or the least;
- how many of the last 30 days you have weighed yourself.
